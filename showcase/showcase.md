# Carom

Carom is a fast top-down paddle duel where every return is a choice. Start a rally, read the angle, and use the walls and gold obstacles to send the ball somewhere your opponent cannot reach.

Moving as you strike adds spin. A little motion bends a safe return into a bank shot; a committed swing can turn a corner and change the whole rally. The glowing trail makes that curve easy to read.

Play solo against the reactive opponent or face a friend locally. The score races to eleven with a two-point lead, so match point is never quite over until the final clean pass.
